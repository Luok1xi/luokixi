Codex's three motion pieces are well engineered. (1) The opening plays once per tab session and runs only on WAAPI. (2) The Collins fan carousel uses a closed-form spring with response .42 and damping .85, samples it into linear WAAPI keyframes, can be interrupted while keeping its velocity, and supports pointer drag with rubber-banding and many pause reasons. (3) The story dialog does a live-DOM FLIP. Everything under the hero is static. The default 推荐 feed has 0 reveal elements (measured), and its first module sits at y=936 on a 1280×800 screen, so the whole feed is below the fold. Tabs are not sticky. Bag, quick-entry and row actions give no feedback beyond the global press scale. Measured gaps: (a) Trackpad horizontal swipes do nothing: ten 60px deltaX wheel events left the index at 0. (b) Arrow keys jump with no animation (0 animations), while clicks animate. (c) The autoplay progress fill is only 8×8px inside a 24px dot, and hovering resets it. (d) The opening's chip-to-nav handoff can't be seen: chip opacity falls 1→0.49 within 50ms of leaving, and the overlay is already at 0.73 while the chips travel. (e) The skeleton and the real carousel differ in size, so .td-body jumps 214px on desktop and 48px on mobile when the hero mounts, which shows on repeat visits. (f) Story detail: the copy jumps from the bottom of the card to the top of the hero, and the title rewraps. This happens because story.css uses space-between and a 780px width, while the card uses flex-end and 860px. The see-through body fade also lets the carousel dots show through mid-open. (g) The tab underline still animates width. (h) Switching tabs stacks a 36px X spring on a 1s/30px Y data-reveal. (i) The whole picks panel waits on Promise.all, including 18 HEAD probes of PDFs with a 3s timeout. With PDF responses slowed to 2.5s the panel appeared at 2795ms, against 319ms for the hero. No console errors except the expected 502/aborted /api calls and the unreachable official news photo; the backend is offline and the sandbox blocks the photo host. GSAP 3.15 with all plugins is installed, but today.js loads none of it. CSS scroll timelines (.as-reveal, already in motion.css) and WAAPI cover most needs more cheaply. GSAP adds real value only for SplitText headline masks, and Observer is an option for wheel and gesture input.
