SUMMARY: These three pages share a sound global motion layer: View Transitions between pages (motion.css:16-70), CSS dialog entry and exit using @starting-style plus overlay allow-discrete (motion.css:75-147), a global press scale (motion.css:151-175), scroll-driven .as-reveal sections, a spring-driven segment thumb, and the fx.js helpers pop() and rollTo() (the like-count number swap). All of it follows the transform/opacity rule. Interactions inside a page are weaker. Every state change is a full innerHTML re-render: materials rebuilds the whole rail and list on each add, remove, filter or search, and 10 rail art icons re-fade on every click. The circle feed, reputation reviews, bag list and upload list all swap with no transition, and Playwright found 0 nodes kept between renders in every case. Like and star are not optimistic: the circle like waits for the server (452ms measured at 400ms latency) and disables the button. The thread sheet opens only after its fetch finishes (842ms measured), and its card-to-sheet FLIP starts at opacity 0.5, so the page shows through the sheet for about 460ms (double exposure). ESC closes it with no animation. Backdrop clicks close nothing on circle or reputation. Feedback often lands off-screen: #rp-message measured at -789px while the user is at #reviews. On mobile the global publish FAB covers the materials 去打包 button. The rating histogram bars never animate, even though fx.js already adds .as-ratings.is-in for them. Review-card and info-strip stars render gray (a CSS specificity bug), so ratings are hard to read. Like, star and toast logic is copied across circle.js, reputation.js, discover.js, map.js and project.js. GSAP (Flip, Draggable, Inertia, MotionPath, DrawSVG) is installed but not used on these pages. Test setup: Vite on :5183 at 1280x800 and 390x844. A scratch-only mock of /api/hub (not part of the site) was used so the interactions could actually run; the real offline state was also captured. No console errors other than the expected backend 502/503 and the external news image failing behind the proxy. No horizontal overflow.
